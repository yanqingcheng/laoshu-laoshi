`learning-data.json → data/course/` contains the existing Laoshu HSK 1/2 curriculum: 26 lesson lists, 326 unique words and 1,028 authored example sentences. These are prepared reference data, not content created during the hackathon. `learning-data.json → data/compounds.json` is the project's seeded transparent/opaque classification table; it is a policy input, not an independently validated linguistic gold standard.

`references/` contains Laoshu cast identity pictures, generated style boards and prior visual mockups supplied with the planning work. They are design references. `references/README.md` identifies their intended use and the obsolete town-camera angle. They are not production art to copy into tonight's submission.
