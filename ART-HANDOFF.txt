DELIVERY MAP
public/art/asset-manifest.json: actual URLs, dimensions, alpha checks, optical bounds,
PNG hashes and encoded byte sizes. WebP cutouts are lossless with identical alpha;
opaque scenes use quality90, with smaller derivatives for narrow displays.
Small cutout variants are also provided (128px markers, 384px objects, 512px cast
and houses, 768px plant canvases). Prefer small_url at those display sizes; keep
full-canvas aspect ratio for plant layers. Original PNGs remain unchanged.
docs/art/START-HERE.txt and ART-DIRECTION.txt: overview and state/motion rules.
docs/art/ART-INDEX.csv: every file, its purpose and intended placement.
docs/art/previews/: labelled placement examples; do not use as production backdrops.
docs/art/annotations/: detailed dimensions, crop rules, state mappings and prompts.
docs/art/prompt-library/: 57 exact past prompts, 7 reusable versioned templates.

DESKTOP AND MOBILE ACCEPTANCE
- Scene buttons and accessible action lists must invoke the same working actions.
- Entire room visible at native 3:2; do not cover important objects with long labels.
- At 360/390px, prefer concise hotspot labels and full-width action list below.
- At 768/1280/1440px, constrain text lines and keep scene plus activity visually balanced.
- Touch targets >=48px; do not shrink controls to match tiny icon pixels.
- Native ruby stays with each word, Hanzi 28-32px/pinyin13-15px where appropriate.
- Test 200% text zoom, long titles, safe-area padding, keyboard, and reduced motion.
- Source-specific scripts and app text remain real UI; never bake captions into art.
- Lazy-load offscreen art; prefer WebP URLs. Fetch only selected plant state and
  preload the next likely state; do not eagerly load all 56 full-size originals.
- Validate against real rendered views, not just these static composition previews.
