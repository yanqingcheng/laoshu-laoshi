LESSON SCENARIO PACKS — EDITORIAL PLANS, NOT GENERATED LESSON CONTENT

Scope: one detailed scenario pack for each of the 26 lessons in src/data/course.json.
These plans extend the existing mouse/dog/cat rooms, shop and park with chapters;
they do not create 26 additional map slots or change curriculum order.

Each pack contains the exact lesson key/order/target words, a motivated scenario,
earlier review words, a four-page story, episode beats, a language-essential game,
a guided conversation, vocabulary-linked objects, art briefs and two viewport plans.
English describes intent. It is not learner-facing Mandarin and must not bypass
the checked generation pipeline. Exact source words are identifiers, not scripts.

HOW TO TURN A PACK INTO CONTENT
1. Resolve lessonKey through the real curriculum; never match by display title alone.
2. Freeze the generation snapshot: NEW targets, prior BRING IN words and explicitly
   allowed words/names. For shared bundled content use the appropriate course prefix.
   For personalized content use actual repertoire and approved source-level rules.
3. Plan natural Mandarin sentences/tokens from the frozen set. Keep the narrative
   motivation and payoff; reduce the words, not the story's interest. At early levels,
   imagery can carry an unspoken action that the language cannot yet describe.
4. Validate all learner-facing tokens through checkText, including choices, game
   instructions, labels, captions, conversation options and intended answers.
   A proposed earlier word is available only if the real learner actually has it.
5. Generate character-consistent illustrations from accepted art briefs using
   docs/art/prompt-library/templates.json. Reuse existing props when they match.
   Log prompts before calls; keep text out of rasters. No generation calls have been
   made for these packs merely by saving this plan.
6. Perform independent narrative and linguistic review, bounded repairs, then store
   ContentItem records and exact requiredWordIds. Never mark an English plan ready.
7. The chooser alone serves eligible content. A bundled place opening at 80% does
   not imply that every item in it is eligible. Retain missing-word and pending states.

EVIDENCE AND REWARDS
Read/episode completion records seen/completed, not automatic recall grades.
Games follow their existing evidence contract; do not invent success as word mastery.
Conversation evidence follows only approved production rules and confirmed answers.
Coins and objects require real committed transactions. A pictured object is not owned.
No extra content gate blocks foundational learning or the accessible action list.

NARRATIVE BAR
A character wants something; a concrete obstacle makes the language useful; a choice
changes what happens; the ending pays off a visual or verbal setup. Avoid vocabulary
parades, disconnected errands, humiliating mistakes and generic congratulation screens.
Keep a recurring cast and a living village, with warm callbacks to earlier chapters.
The player must read/listen to distinguish choices; identical colours or matching
pictures cannot replace understanding the Chinese instruction.

DESKTOP AND MOBILE
See responsive-ux.json for shared layout/interaction acceptance, then each pack's
desktopUX/mobileUX for scene-specific decisions. Preview compositions are plans;
browser screenshots and interaction checks must happen after app implementation.
Test with exact checked Mandarin, ruby, longest choices and real safe-area keyboards.

FILES

index.json and LESSON-INDEX.csv: catalogue and coverage.
validation-report.json: source-word, reference and structure checks; never a claim
of linguistic validation, playable games, generated art or completed browser QA.
