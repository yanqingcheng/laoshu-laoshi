WHERE EACH PIECE GOES
prompt-library/ holds 57 exact historical generation prompts (including retries),
seven versioned on-demand templates, and the append-only workflow for future calls.
ART-INDEX.csv is a plain catalogue: each file, its purpose, where it goes and its state.
asset-manifest.json has measured sizes, transparency, optical bounds and annotation links.
ART-DIRECTION.txt explains the visual rules, motion suggestions and integration semantics.
annotations/plants.json has the exact shared plant placement and state mappings.
annotations/placement-recipes.json has suggested object locations and marker rules.

IMPORTANT FOR LOVABLE
The PNGs are static. Animate markers and care props in the app using the supplied motion
suggestions; use static alternatives for reduced motion. Connect plants and markers to
actual saved state. No fake completion, ownership, pending job or streak is baked in.
Use plants/home-room-empty-windowsill.png for a dynamic home room; do not overlay plants
on core/home-room.png, which already contains painted plants.
Plant images are registered to one shared canvas: do not trim individual plant states.
Small objects and markers can be fitted using their visible bounds; preserve aspect ratio.
All positions are artist suggestions from inspected images; validate mobile layout and
touch targets in the app. The original furniture hotspots remain available in core.

SCOPE
This folder is ready for integration, not an already-integrated app. No app source exists
locally yet. Personal stories, neighbours, lesson pictures and game-specific art remain
runtime generation from the learner's approved vocabulary; see ART-DIRECTION.txt.
