PLANTS
Growth and care condition are separate axes. Seedling, growing and thriving reflect
accumulated care. Each has healthy, drooping and recovering artwork. Missing days
does not reset maturity. Plants never die. Return and care are welcoming.
Map the real saved review/care state to these images. No visible numeric streak.
Do not infer completion just because an animation played. Exact thresholds belong
to the app's selected product rules; historical rehearsal numbers are not mandates.
Remove the baked plants by using the new empty-windowsill room BEFORE layering the
trio. Do not place a second plant layer on top of the old painted-in plants.
Keep all states on the same registered canvas and replace/crossfade the entire trio.

MARKERS
Canonical map priority: empty plot; otherwise locked if not eligible; otherwise cog
if open but generation pending; otherwise ! if unseen eligible content; otherwise none.
Question mark means help. NEW starburst is optional decorative emphasis, not another
eligibility state. A construction barrier means actual construction/job work, not a
vocabulary lock. A failed job should show Retry, never perpetual construction.
Render accessible names and status words as real UI text. No marker alone explains
an error. Ordinary keyboard focus, text labels and button hit areas remain app-owned.

MOTION SUGGESTIONS (not baked into PNGs)
Unseen: gentle 2px bob over 2.4 seconds; stop after a few cycles or on interaction.
Pending cog: slow 6-second rotation only while a job is actually pending.
New sparkle: one brief opacity/scale pulse on arrival, not continuous flashing.
Watering: can tilts slightly; droplets travel toward plants; state crossfade 350ms.
Recovery: a brief sparkle, then static restored plants. No camera shake or punishment.
Reduced motion: static markers, instant or short opacity-only state changes.
Marker art at roughly 28-40px; touch targets at least 48px, independent of art size.

LANDING / HOME PAGE
Desktop hero reserves a quiet left zone for title, short explanation and primary action.
Mobile hero is separately composed; do not crop the desktop scene to a narrow slice.
The trio belongs on landing/onboarding/welcome screens. The teacher does not live in
the learner's personal room. Keep real title and sign-in controls outside the raster.

COLLECTIBLES AND WALL ART
These are reusable starter art assets, not evidence an item is owned or purchasable.
The app associates canonical word IDs and only offers items allowed by actual vocabulary.
Coins reflect committed practice; they are distinct from generation credits.
Frames/scrolls stay blank so correct Hanzi/pinyin can be rendered as real selectable text.
Do not invent glyphs inside generated paintings. Place small objects on the shelf/table,
floor items on the floor, hanging art on clear wall. Store normalized Layout positions.

RUNTIME ART VS FIXED ART
Personal neighbours, source-specific houses, room themes, lesson images, book scenes,
comic panels, and game-specific items depend on the learner and approved vocabulary.
They remain runtime generation tasks. Fixed art here supplies reusable visual foundations;
no stock placeholder should be claimed as a newly generated personal scene.
Shop uses stock house/room per event spec. Park/gym uses its existing map slot; unique
future arcade/cafe/grammar buildings are not required to invent during this art pass.

QUALITY / DELIVERY
Keep original PNG alpha; never erase cream pixels to remove a background.
Check foreground trim bounds before placing objects; transparent padding is significant.
All originals and rejected attempts are preserved outside this delivery folder.
Detailed prompts and measured times are included; model IDs and cost are unknown when
not exposed by the built-in image tool. No reference images, secrets, or app code ship.
