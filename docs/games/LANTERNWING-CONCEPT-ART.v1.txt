LANTERNWING — concept and art direction v1, 2026-10-06
Requested model: gpt-6-astra, max reasoning. Actual model, usage and cost metadata
are not exposed to this artifact author; record null rather than infer billing.

Six play choices considered before implementation
1. Lanternwing: directly steer a mouse's leaf glider through drifting fireflies,
   collect exactly the Mandarin request, then send them to power a paper lantern.
   Twenty seconds: read an untimed request, launch, curve past a rain cloud, catch
   three lights, deliberately discard an accidental extra, launch the lantern.
   Mandarin determines when the inventory is right; geometry determines the route.
2. Moon-mine cart: move a cart between switching rails, collect exact ore counts.
   Good physical causality, but phone rail junctions can turn into answer lanes.
3. Rooftop hop: aim a mouse between platforms and stop after the requested count.
   Strong movement, but counting jumps competes with reading and missed jumps.
4. Tide-pool gardener: swim a small turtle collecting requested seed quantities.
   Navigation and currents work; delivering back to an island is too close to the
   rejected ferry fantasy and risks unnecessary walking.
5. Clockwork fireworks: turn numbered gears then aim fireworks at moving targets.
   Spectacular payoff, but planning gears can resemble a static answer panel.
6. Balancing acrobats: drag cat/mouse performers onto a seesaw, use requested
   counts to balance a moving troupe. Charming, harder to teach without extra
   vocabulary; multiple balancing rules compete with this early numbers lesson.

Selection: Lanternwing. It has a immediately visible cause and reward: capture
small lights, pour the exact number into a giant lantern, watch a new lantern join
the festival. No three-answer grid or coloured answer lane. The player directly
steers with one-finger tap/drag or arrows. Space sends the cargo; Backspace/X lets
one firefly go. Ten requests use each real course number once. The first request
is a guided three; remaining requests are unscaffolded and deliberately unordered.
Calendar vocabulary is explicitly deferred. No fake calendar coverage claims.

Visual composition — actually draw these
An illustrated dusk paper theatre, cream-lit violet sky, peach horizon, layered
teal mountain islands and indigo foreground eaves. Fine paper flecks, curved cloud
cutouts, hanging string lights and dim distant windows create depth. No emoji.
The stage is framed by a branch with paper pennants at top left and a carved
festival platform at bottom. The central flight space stays visually open.
The hero is a recognisable cream mouse: two large pink inner ears, long snout,
black bead eyes, copper aviator goggles, curved tail, coral scarf with two flowing
ends, tiny gripping paws, in a curled jade leaf glider with visible ribs and cord
rigging. The body tilts, the leaf rolls, scarf trails, ears bounce and paws squash
when collecting. Its caught lights orbit in a little glass belly lantern.
Fireflies have gold abdomens, dark heads, two paper-thin wings and dotted trails.
Cloud obstacles are soft blue sculpted rain puffs with falling teardrops and
sleepy faces. Contact releases one light and deflects the glider, with invulnerability;
it does not punish reading by costing a life. More clouds appear after tutorial.
The delivery lantern is a large apricot ribbed hot-air paper lantern with brass
rim, tassel, visible candle, scalloped seams and a warm pool of light. On success,
fireflies stream from the glider to its candle, it inflates, rises, becomes one of
the distant festival lights, and a new unlit lantern swings into place.
Misses make the lantern gently droop and flash a clear crossed count comparison.
Corrected Chinese plus explicit quantity appears only after a committed mistake.
Victory sends the entire lantern parade through the sky with paper confetti,
mouse salute, extra little mouse spectators peeking over the lower eaves.

Motion and actual decisions
Single RAF loop, real clamped delta time. Inertial steering with spring smoothing;
fireflies drift slowly along curved deterministic paths. Player chooses routes
through an open arena, avoids clouds and chooses when to stop collecting. Inventory
caps at ten. Cargo is shown as collected quantity, never a translated target.
Each round begins with an untimed read/rest phase; only explicit play starts motion.
Pickup streams never run out. Three wrong deliveries end a run; cloud contact and
overcollection are recoverable. Ten correct deliveries win. Score rewards precise
delivery and successive first tries, never reading speed. Corrections remain until
acknowledged. Reduced motion removes parallax, shaking and ambient flourishes,
but preserves functional movement with slower hazards and immediate rewards.

Onboarding
The title screen is the real world, not a blank card. It runs a wordless animated
demonstration: the same hero collects three lights, the checked number-three ruby
is shown, a illustrated fingertip presses the lantern control, light enters the
lantern and it rises. Three pictogram beats remain beneath the title. First play
round uses three fixed slow pickups and an explicit illustrated drag cue. The
request stays visible; a dotted path and destination cue are tutorial-only, removed
on round two. A persistent ? replays the demonstration, suspending the live run.
Host-facing English how-to is a separate artifact and preview host panel.

Layout and text
Canvas fills frame; responsive logical world uses available arena between fixed
top controls/request and lower cargo/action bar. On 320x480 the scene crops side
mountains, enlarges the mouse and preserves the same 56px controls; desktop opens
the side scenery. No whole-scene scaling that shrinks text. Every Chinese surface
calls the injected renderer; Hanzi 28px and pinyin 14px native ruby are untouched.
All text arrives in parent-only init; source contains no Chinese/pinyin literals.

Evidence requirements
First preserve static proof made by the shipping draw routine, then screenshot
three viewport sizes. Exercise public controls only: complete win, three mistakes,
discard, restart, duplicate init, keyboard/touch and help/reduced motion. Record
production checkText and independent blind play as outstanding if unavailable.
This handcrafted experiment is not an unattended pipeline acceptance claim.
